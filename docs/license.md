## What "non-commercial" means here

Teaching, learning, research, and use inside a school, college or university are all welcome — including where that institution charges tuition. Selling this material, putting it behind a paywall, or using it in paid training or consulting is not.

If your situation sits somewhere between the two, write and ask. The answer is usually yes.

## Why some lab material needs a code

Each lab has four stages. The **Student Handout** and the **Data Acquisition** instructions are open to everyone: anyone can read the scenario and go and fetch the data.

The **Project Instructions** and the **Result Reference** are not. They are live assessment material for students taking the course, and publishing them openly would make every lab useless as coursework — for my students and for anyone else teaching from this site.

!!! note "A courtesy, not a lock"
    The access code is a request, not security. It keeps the answers from appearing in front of someone who came for the handout; it is not a serious barrier to anyone determined to get past it. It works because people respect what it is for.

**If you are an instructor** and you would like the project instructions and reference answers for your own teaching, please write and ask. They are given freely for that purpose.

**If you are a student** on a course that uses this site, your instructor has the code.
