# About

<p class="lede">This site is an introduction to Geographic Information Systems, taught through ArcGIS Pro.</p>

</div>

It was built for graduate students at the CUNY Graduate Center and for students across every CUNY campus in New York City, and anyone else who would like to learn is welcome here too. All of the content on this site, including the lessons, labs, slides, and datasets, is free and open access.

The site is created and maintained by Shokran Rahiminezhad, a PhD candidate in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences at the CUNY Graduate Center and an adjunct lecturer at Hunter College and Lehman College, where he teaches Geographic Information Systems.

If you have a question, notice something that needs correcting, or would like to see a topic covered, you are always welcome to write to him at [srahiminezhadlotfaba@gradcenter.cuny.edu](mailto:srahiminezhadlotfaba@gradcenter.cuny.edu).

The lessons, labs and design of this site are published under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](license.md): use them, adapt them, teach from them, credit the author, and keep them non-commercial. Two stages of each lab — the project instructions and the reference answers — are live assessment material and need an access code; [instructors are welcome to ask for it](license.md#why-some-lab-material-needs-a-code).
